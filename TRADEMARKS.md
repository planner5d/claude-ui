## Forks and redistribution

Apache-2.0 permits forks and redistribution under its terms. Retain the license
and applicable copyright and attribution notices, including
[NOTICE.txt](NOTICE.txt), as required by section 4.

For an independently published fork, use your own plugin name, display name,
publisher identity and icon. Identify its relationship to the original project
accurately. Claude's [plugin submission checklist](https://claude.com/docs/plugins/pre-submission-checklist#manifest-and-plugin-name)
requires forks to have their own name and flags misleading publisher branding.

These branding guidelines do not add restrictions to the Apache-2.0 copyright
or patent grants. They address identification and trademark use.
